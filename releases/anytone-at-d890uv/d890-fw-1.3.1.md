The 1.3.1 release of the AT-D890UV firmware mods. It applies to the stock **V1.05** package (20260521) only. Everything in 1.3.0 is also in this build.

**What's new**

- **Up/Down keys setting:** Settings > Radio Set > Key Func has a new last row, **Up/Down**. Choose what Up and Down do on the channel screen: **Zone** (the default), **Channel** (like the channel knob) or **Talk group** (like TG next and TG previous, with no key to assign in the CPS). Up goes to the next one and Down to the previous one, whichever value you choose, the zone included: this is the opposite way round to the stock zone keys. With Channel or Talk group the "Zone" label and its arrows are no longer drawn at the bottom of the channel screen. A running scan, a long press (which starts a scan), the VFO, popups and the keypad lock are as before. The setting is kept over a power-off.
- **Reply on the call screen:** while a call is on the receive screen, the right-hand soft key says **Reply** instead of List. Press it to answer: the radio goes back to the channel screen with the call's talk group (or, for a private call, the caller) as the target for PTT, on the timeslot the call came in on. It works without a TG key assigned, lasts until the next talk group step or channel change, and saves nothing. An all call, a call to or from ID 0, a call on the sub side, a call while scanning, VFO and NXDN keep the stock List key.
- **Quick filters during a call:** the quick filters menu now opens while a call is being received, on the call screen, and stays open if a call starts. Set a filter and close it to be back on the call screen, on the same channel. RX match, TS filter and TG slot restart the receiver, so the current call may drop. Pressing PTT closes the menu. On the heard-call card of a zone scan, the quick filters key stops the scan on that channel and opens the menu.
- **S-Meter screen and Signal graph during a call and while you transmit:** both screens can now be opened while a call is being received (from the menu, or with the S-Meter key), and a call that starts or ends no longer closes them. They also stay up when you press PTT: the S-Meter screen shows TX with a solid magenta bar, and the Signal graph keeps its time axis and marks each column taken while you transmit as a full-height magenta bar, so you can see how long the over was. Both carry on with live readings when you let go. The Spectrum is unchanged: it still closes when you transmit and does not open during a call.

**What's changed**

- **Quieter bottom of the volume ranges:** at knob level 1 the Indoor range now starts at -42 dB (it was -39 dB) and the Outdoor range at -30 dB (it was -24 dB). The tops of the ranges are unchanged, so the steps between are a little larger. Full and Stock are unchanged.
- **Time Slot in the channel menu takes effect at once:** editing **Time Slot** while a `*` or talk-group timeslot is in use now switches the radio to the slot you picked straight away (before, it only changed the saved channel until you changed channel). A Time Slot edit in the channel menu pins that slot, with or without `*`, until the next TG next / previous or channel change.
- **Timeslots in more places follow the slot in use:** on a TDMA channel, PTT no longer disturbs a call on the other timeslot when `*` or a talk group's slot is in use, and the digital APRS report keeps its own timeslot. Roaming's explicit timeslots are unchanged.

**Known limits**

- The volume ranges' numbers are still provisional until they are tuned on a radio.
- Changing RX match, TS filter or TG slot from the quick filters during a call can drop the call for a moment.
- The Reply label is in English in every menu language.

**Checking it worked:** after the radio restarts, Device Info > Mod Ver shows `mm9pdy-1.3.1`. Settings > Radio Set > Key Func ends with **Up/Down**.

**Going back to stock:** flash stock V1.05 from this page, or use the official CPS.
