The 1.2.0 release of the AT-D890UV firmware mods. It applies to the stock **V1.05** package (20260521) only.

It is the same build as release candidate 1.2.0-rc.4, apart from the release name, and rc.4 passed its radio check. Everything in 1.1.0 is also in this build.

**What's new since 1.1.0**

- **Channel distance:** the channel screen shows how far away the current memory channel's site is, and in which direction, from the radio's GPS fix, for example `850m N` or `4.3km NE`. It needs channel positions written by Codeplug Studio's **MM9PDY** target, GPS on and a recent fix.
- **Single display:** one 3-line layout for analogue and digital channels: the channel name, then the talk group or receive frequency, then the zone name on a small line underneath. In the dual display the sub side's line shows its own `Talk group / Zone`.
- **S-meter:** Main Menu > **S-Meter** opens the screens first, then the settings.
  - A signal bar on the channel screen, coloured by level, with a raw, dBm or S-unit reading.
  - A full-screen meter with min / max / average and a difference mode for comparing antennas.
  - A **spectrum** that sweeps the receiver around the channel and shows signals as peaks at their own frequency.
  - A **signal history** that plots the channel's level over time, like a heart monitor.
  - One fixed colour scale everywhere, so a colour always means the same strength.
- **Zone scan resume time:** Main Menu > Scan > **Zone scan resume** (0.5 s, 1 s, 2 s, 5 s or 10 s; default 5 s) sets how long a zone scan waits on a channel after the activity ends, and after you transmit, before it carries on. Before, it could carry on after a fraction of a second.
- **Heard-call popup:** a zone scan that holds on a DMR call shows who and which talk group. **Scan** (Zone / List) and **Scan popup** are rows of Main Menu > Scan.
- **Timeslots:** the T1 / T2 badge shows the slot in use, a short `*` always switches it, and a Slot Switch key does the same and saves it into the channel.
- **Volume:** **Vol Range** (Stock, Indoor, Outdoor, Full) changes how the knob maps to loudness, with true silence at the bottom of a range. An optional volume bar shows the level and range when you turn the knob; press **Menu** on it to step the range. Both settings are rows of Settings > Radio Set > Voice Func. With a Bluetooth headset connected, the knob sets the headset's volume.
- **Quick filters:** **Tone match: On / Off** (Off opens the squelch of a toned analogue channel on any signal) and **To VFO: Copy** (copies the main side's channel into its VFO).

**Known limits**

- The channel distance text can clip at the right, and the dual display shows no distance.
- The S-meter's dBm, S-units and colours are approximate until it is calibrated. On the air band the signal bar stays on screen with its last reading. During a spectrum sweep the main side isn't listening to the channel.
- The volume ranges' loudness steps are first values and may change in a later release.
- A channel with no contact must store "none" within 0–9999 (Codeplug Studio writes 9999).

**Checking it worked:** after the radio restarts, Device Info > Mod Ver shows `mm9pdy-1.2.0`.

**Going back to stock:** flash stock V1.05 from this page, or use the official CPS.
