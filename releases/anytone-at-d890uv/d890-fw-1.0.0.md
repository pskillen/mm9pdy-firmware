The first full release of the AT-D890UV firmware mods. It applies to the stock **V1.05** package (20260521) only.

It is the same build as release candidate 1.0.0-rc.3, apart from the release name, and that build passed its full
radio check.

**What's in it**

- **Talk-group keys:** step through the channel's RX group list with TG Previous / TG Next, and transmit on the picked
  talk group. A `TS1` or `TS2` suffix on a talk-group name sets the timeslot.
- **Star-key timeslot toggle.**
- **Quick filters menu** with five rows: RX match (TG / RGL / Any), TS filter, CC match, Scan (Zone / List) and TG slot.
  The settings are kept across a power cycle and across a write with the official CPS.
- **Zone scan:** scans the whole zone, including on channels with no scan list.
- The release name shows on the power-on screen and in Device Info.

**Checking it worked:** after the radio restarts, Device Info > Version shows `1.0.0`.

**Going back to stock:** flash stock V1.05 from this page, or use the official CPS.
