A release candidate for 1.2.0, for testing. It applies to the stock **V1.05** package (20260521) only. It is 1.2.0-rc.2 with the **S-meter** added. Everything in 1.1.0 and 1.2.0-rc.2 is also in this build.

**How far it has been checked:** the S-meter has been tried on the owner's radio from a test build: the bar, the signal screen and the spectrum all respond to a nearby transmitter. This exact build is checked in an emulator only.

**What's new since 1.2.0-rc.2**

- **S-meter menu:** the Main Menu has a new **S-Meter** item at the end. **Display** turns the signal bar on and picks what it shows: Bar, Bar + raw, Bar + dBm or Bar + S (S-units). It is off by default. **Average** smooths the reading (Off, Fast, Slow). **Peak hold** keeps a peak marker for 1 or 3 seconds.
- **Signal bar:** in the single display, a bar under the channel shows the received signal strength, with the reading in the corner. It replaces the last-call lines while it is on. It shows the noise floor as well as signals, so it moves even when nothing is heard.
- **Signal screen:** **S-Meter > S-Meter screen** (or a key assigned to **Satellite Predicting** in the CPS, which this firmware turns into the S-meter key) opens a full-screen meter with a large reading, a scale, and min / max / average. Press **P1** to take the current reading as a reference: the screen then shows the difference in dB, which is handy for comparing antennas. **P2** clears the reference and the min / max. Up and Down change the unit. **Back** leaves it.
- **Spectrum:** **S-Meter > Spectrum** sweeps the main side's receiver across a band around the channel and draws the signal level at each step. **S-Meter > Spectrum set** chooses the span, the step and the top and bottom of the scale. On the screen, **P1** and **P2** change the span and step, Up and Down move a marker that shows its frequency and level, and **Back** stops the sweep and returns the radio to the channel. The backlight stays on while the spectrum is open.

**Known limits**

- The dBm and S-unit readings are approximate: the meter isn't calibrated against a signal generator yet. The raw reading and the dB difference on the signal screen are the reliable parts, so use those to compare antennas.
- On the air band (108–136 MHz) the radio can't measure signal strength. The spectrum won't start there, but the signal bar stays on screen with its last reading. That is a bug, to be fixed.
- The dual display shows no signal bar.
- The spectrum sweeps a few dozen steps a second, and stays within the channel's band.
- During a sweep the main side isn't listening to the channel. Back returns it.

**Checking it worked:** after the radio restarts, Device Info > Mod Ver shows `mm9pdy-1.2.0-rc.3`, and Main Menu ends with **S-Meter**.

**Going back to stock:** flash stock V1.05 from this page, or use the official CPS.
