The 1.1.0 release of the AT-D890UV firmware mods. It applies to the stock **V1.05** package (20260521) only.

It is the same build as release candidate 1.1.0-rc.2, apart from the release name, and rc.2 passed its radio check. Everything in 1.0.0 is also in this build.

**What's new since 1.0.0**

- **Mod version:** the power-on screen shows `mm9pdy-1.1.0`, and Menu > Settings > Device Info has a **Mod Ver:** item with the same text. Firmware Ver: still shows the stock `Ver 1.05`.
- **TG Next / TG Previous during a call:** step off a busy talk group while a call is being received. Main side and digital memory channels only; they do nothing while the sub side is receiving.
- **Stop a scan, reply to what you heard:** stop a zone scan during or just after a DMR call and the radio stays on the talk group you heard, so PTT answers it. A talk group outside the channel's RX group list is used for now only.
- **Channel with no contact:** a digital channel with no transmit contact starts on the first talk group in its RX group list, not on talk-group slot 0. This needs TG Next or TG Previous assigned to a key.
- **A timeslot per RX group list member:** set in Codeplug Studio's MM9PDY target. Step to that talk group and you transmit on its slot. The list's slot wins over a `TS1` / `TS2` name suffix.
- **Talk group and zone on screen:** a digital channel shows the channel name, the talk group, and the zone name in small text underneath. With both channels shown, line 2 reads `Talk group | Zone`.

**Known limits**

- A channel with no contact must store "none" as an unused talk-group slot within 0–9999. Codeplug Studio writes 9999. A contact word above 9999 makes the stock radio show "Program Error" at power-on on that channel.
- Per-member timeslots are written only by Codeplug Studio. A write with the official CPS, or editing that list's members on the radio, is expected to drop them; neither is confirmed on the radio.
- On the dual display, a `Talk group | Zone` pair with a non-ASCII character is cut at 15 characters.
- What a call does straight after a TG step during that call is unconfirmed.

**Checking it worked:** after the radio restarts, Device Info > Mod Ver shows `mm9pdy-1.1.0`.

**Going back to stock:** flash stock V1.05 from this page, or use the official CPS.
