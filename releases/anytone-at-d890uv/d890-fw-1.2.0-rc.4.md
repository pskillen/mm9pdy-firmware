A release candidate for 1.2.0, for testing. It applies to the stock **V1.05** package (20260521) only. It is 1.2.0-rc.3 with the changes below. Everything in 1.1.0 and 1.2.0-rc.3 is also in this build.

**How far it has been checked:** the S-meter changes have been tried on the owner's radio from test builds, and the spectrum fix was confirmed with a second radio transmitting near the channel. The volume bar and its menu rows have been tried on the owner's radio from a test build; the loudness of the volume ranges hasn't been tuned by ear yet. The other changes, and this exact build, have been checked in an emulator only.

**What's new since 1.2.0-rc.3**

- **T1 / T2 badge:** the red badge next to `CH-n` now shows the timeslot the radio is using. A talk group picked with TG next / previous that has its own timeslot (from its RX group list, or a name ending `TS1` / `TS2`) shows that slot; before, the badge always showed the channel's slot. In the dual display, each side's badge follows that side's talk group.
- **Star key always wins:** a short `*` now switches the timeslot the radio is using, even on a talk group with its own timeslot (from its RX group list or a `TS1` / `TS2` name), and "TS n Selected" and the badge name the slot in use. TG next / previous, or changing channel or zone, goes back to the talk group's own slot, or the channel's when the talk group has none.
- **Slot Switch key:** a key set to the radio's own Slot Switch function now switches from the timeslot in use, as `*` does, and saves it into the channel.
- **The spectrum now shows signals where they are.** In rc.3 the receiver didn't actually move away from the channel during a sweep, so the whole graph rose or fell with whatever was on or near the channel, and no peak appeared. Each point now really retunes the receiver, the same way a scan does, and a signal shows as a peak at its own frequency.
- **S-meter colours:** the bar and the spectrum are coloured by signal level on one fixed scale: dark blue for very weak, then blue, cyan, green, yellow and orange, up to red for strong. A colour always means the same strength, whatever span or range you choose. The bar's peak marker is red.
- **Signal history:** **S-Meter > Signal graph** plots the channel's signal level over time, like a heart monitor: one column every 0.1 second, 16 seconds across the screen. A short blank gap marks "now". **P1** pauses and resumes, **P2** clears, **Back** leaves. The radio stays on your channel while it is open.
- **Spectrum gap:** like the signal history, the spectrum shows a short blank gap just ahead of the point being measured, so you can see where the sweep has got to.
- **Zone scan resume time:** Main Menu > Scan has a new **Zone scan resume** row: 0.5 s, 1 s, 2 s, 5 s or 10 s. It is how long a zone scan waits on a channel after the activity ends, and after you transmit, before it carries on. The default is 5 s. Before, a zone scan could carry on after a fraction of a second, so a reply was easy to miss.
- **Scan and Scan popup moved:** these two rows are now in Main Menu > Scan, after the stock rows, and keep the value you had. They are no longer in the quick filters menu.
- **Quick filters: Tone match:** the **Tone** row is now called **Tone match** and shows On (the stock behaviour) or Off (any tone opens the squelch).
- **Volume ranges:** a new setting changes how the volume knob maps to loudness. **Indoor** keeps the low end gentle for quiet rooms, **Outdoor** is louder low down, and **Full** spreads the whole range across the knob. **Stock**, the default, is the release's own loudness, so nothing changes until you choose a range. Under a range, the knob fully down is true silence (no hiss, no beeps), and a knob resting near a step no longer flickers between two levels. The knob still sets the volume; the range only changes what each position means. An earpiece and a Bluetooth headset keep their stock levels.
- **Volume bar:** turning the knob shows a bar with the level and, under it, the range in use (`Range: Stock`, `Indoor`, `Outdoor`, `Full`, or `BT` with a headset). Press **Menu** while the bar is up to step to the next range; the loudness changes at once. The bar closes by itself after a few seconds. It doesn't appear over the heard-call card, the quick filters or the menus.
- **New Voice Func rows:** Settings > Radio Set > Voice Func has two new rows at the end. **Vol Popup** turns the volume bar on or off (it is the CPS's volume prompt option, now on the radio). **Vol Range** picks the range: Stock, Indoor, Outdoor or Full.
- **S-Meter menu order:** the screens come first, then the settings: S-Meter screen, Signal graph, Spectrum, Display, Average, Peak hold, Spectrum set.

**Known limits**

- The dBm and S-unit readings, and so the colours, are approximate: the meter isn't calibrated against a signal generator yet. The raw reading and the dB difference on the signal screen are the reliable parts.
- On the air band (108–136 MHz) the radio can't measure signal strength. The spectrum won't start there and the signal history draws nothing, but the signal bar stays on screen with its last reading. That is a bug, still to be fixed.
- The volume ranges' loudness steps are first values, to be tuned on the radio. Expect them to change in a later build.
- The spectrum measures about 25 points a second and stays within the channel's band. During a sweep the main side isn't listening to the channel; **Back** returns it.

**Checking it worked:** after the radio restarts, Device Info > Mod Ver shows `mm9pdy-1.2.0-rc.4`.
