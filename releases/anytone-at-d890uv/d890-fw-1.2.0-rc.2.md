A release candidate for 1.2.0, for testing. It applies to the stock **V1.05** package (20260521) only. It is 1.2.0-rc.1 with **channel distance** added. Everything in 1.1.0 is also in this build.

**How far it has been checked:** this build has been written to the owner's radio. A Codeplug Studio write worked and the channel distances showed. The other 1.2.0 changes below are checked in an emulator only.

**What's new since 1.1.0**

- **Channel distance:** the channel screen shows how far away the current memory channel's site is, and in which direction, from the radio's GPS fix, for example `850m N` or `4.3km NE`. It needs channel positions written by Codeplug Studio's **MM9PDY** target, GPS on and a recent fix. VFO, scanning and channels with no position show nothing. A channel renamed or retuned on the radio shows nothing until Studio writes it again.
- **Single display:** one 3-line layout for analogue and digital channels: the channel name, then the talk group or receive frequency, then the zone name on a small line underneath.
- **Dual display:** the sub side's line shows the sub side's own talk group, `Talk group / Zone`.
- **Heard-call popup:** a zone scan that holds on a DMR call shows a box over the receive screen. It is on by default; the quick filters' new **Scan popup** row turns it off.
- **Bluetooth headset volume:** while a headset is connected, the volume knob sets its volume.
- **Quick filters Tone: Ch / Any:** Any opens the squelch of an analogue channel with a receive tone on any signal. The channel still transmits its tone.
- **Quick filters To VFO: Copy:** copies the main side's channel into that side's VFO and switches to it.

The quick filters list now has eight rows and scrolls.

**Known limits**

- The distance text can clip.
- The dual display shows no distance, on either side.
- Whether the Bluetooth module applies the knob's volume on a live link is unchecked; some headsets keep their own volume.
- A channel with no contact must store "none" within 0–9999 (Codeplug Studio writes 9999).

**Checking it worked:** after the radio restarts, Device Info > Mod Ver shows `mm9pdy-1.2.0-rc.2`.
